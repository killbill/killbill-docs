== Overview

The AI Assistant in the Aviate catalog drafts products and plans from a plain-English description. Instead of filling in each field, you describe what you want to create. The assistant then prepares the product and the plan for you, including the trial phase and the recurring price.

You can ask for:

* a new product,
* a plan for an existing product, or
* both.

The assistant only prepares a draft. The changes are marked *Unsaved*, and nothing goes live until you click *Publish*.

== Before you start

* You have access to Aviate, and a Kill Bill deployment has been added. See the https://docs.killbill.io/latest/aviate-deployment-management.html[Aviate Deployment Management] guide.
* You know how products and plans work in the Aviate catalog. See the https://docs.killbill.io/latest/aviate-catalog-guide.html[Aviate Catalog Guide].

== Use the AI Assistant

. In Aviate, go to the *Catalog* page.
. Click the sparkle button next to *Add product*. Its tooltip reads *Add product and plans with AI assistant*.
+
The *AI Assistant* opens.
. Describe in plain English what you want to create. For example:
+
----
A monthly plan with a 14-day free trial
----
+
TIP: Mention the pricing, the billing period and the trial length in your description.
+
The assistant drafts the product and the plan, including the trial phase and the recurring price.

== Review and publish

The drafted changes appear in the catalog marked *Unsaved*. A banner asks you to review the changes, then publish them.

* To make the changes live, click *Publish*.
* To discard the changes, click *Cancel*. Nothing is published.

Nothing goes live until you click *Publish*.

TIP: Check the product name, the plan, the trial length, the billing period and the price before you click *Publish*.

== Advanced: paste intent JSON

The AI Assistant also offers an advanced option, *Paste intent JSON*. It accepts a structured definition of what to create, instead of a plain-English description.
