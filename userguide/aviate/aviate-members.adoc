== Overview

An Aviate subscription is not limited to the person who bought it. The *owner* of the subscription creates an *organization* and invites *members*: colleagues from sales, finance, support or operations who work in Aviate with their own login. Each member has a *role*, which decides what they can see and change.

This guide explains how to set up your organization, invite members, change their role and revoke their access.

== Key Concepts

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Term | Meaning

| Owner
| The person who holds the Aviate subscription. The owner always has the `aviate_owner` role, which grants every permission. Only the owner manages members.

| Organization
| The owner's team. It has a unique name, chosen by the owner.

| Member
| A person the owner invited. A member uses the Aviate features included in the organization's plan, with their own Aviate login.

| Role
| A Kill Bill role that lists what the member can do, in Kill Bill and in Aviate. New members get `aviate_read` (read-only) unless the owner picks another role. See https://docs.killbill.io/latest/aviate-access-control.html[Aviate Access Control].
|===

Members never see or use the owner's Kill Bill credentials: they connect to Kill Bill with their own Aviate login, and Kill Bill applies their role. This requires a Kill Bill deployment that accepts Aviate tokens (see https://docs.killbill.io/latest/aviate-access-control.html#_step_1_accept_aviate_tokens_in_kill_bill[Accept Aviate Tokens in Kill Bill]). On a deployment that doesn't, Aviate asks the member for their own Kill Bill username and password.

== Prerequisites

* An Aviate subscription. You must be its owner.
* Access control enabled on each Kill Bill deployment your organization uses, and the roles you plan to give members defined there. See https://docs.killbill.io/latest/aviate-access-control.html#_enabling_access_control[Enabling Access Control]. Deployments hosted by Aviate are already set up.

== Step 1: Create Your Organization

. Sign in to https://aviate.killbill.io[Aviate].
. Open your profile from the account menu, and select the *Members* tab.
. Enter a unique *Organisation name*, for example `acme-corp`, and click *Create organisation*.

You only do this once. The *Members* tab then lists your members.

== Step 2: Invite a Member

. On the *Members* tab, click *Invite member*.
. Enter the person's *Email address*.
. Pick a *Role*. Aviate lists the roles defined on the Kill Bill of your current deployment. If it cannot read them, type the role name. Leave it empty to give `aviate_read`.
. Click *Send invitation*.

The member appears with the status *Pending*.

[NOTE]
The role must exist on *every* Kill Bill deployment the member uses. On a deployment where it does not exist, the member can read Kill Bill data but cannot read Aviate data or change anything.

== Step 3: The Member Accepts

Aviate does not send an email: let the person know you invited them. Then they:

. Sign in to https://aviate.killbill.io[Aviate] with the email address you invited, or sign up with it if they don't have an Aviate login yet.
. Open their profile from the account menu, and select the *Invitations* tab.
. Accept the invitation from your organization.

Their status becomes *Active*. If they decline, it becomes *Declined*.

If your organization uses single sign-on, there is nothing to accept: the member's first sign-in through your identity provider accepts the invitation. See https://docs.killbill.io/latest/aviate-sso.html[Aviate Single Sign-On].

== Change a Member's Role

On the *Members* tab, pick a new role in the *Role* column of a *Pending* or *Active* member. The change is saved at once, and applies the next time the member's session is refreshed.

A role that is not defined on the Kill Bill of your current deployment is shown as _(not in this Kill Bill)_.

== Revoke Access

On the *Members* tab, click the revoke button (*×*) of the member and confirm. The member immediately loses access to your organization, and their status becomes *Revoked*.

== Member Statuses

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Status | Meaning

| Pending
| Invited, has not accepted yet. You can change the role or revoke the invitation.

| Active
| Accepted the invitation, or joined through single sign-on.

| Declined
| Declined the invitation.

| Revoked
| Access revoked by the owner.
|===

== What Members Can Do

* Use the Aviate features included in your organization's plan, within the limits of their role.
* Add and manage their own Kill Bill deployments in Aviate. Deployments are saved per user, in the browser, and are not shared with the rest of the organization.
* Call the Aviate and Kill Bill APIs with their own ID token. See https://docs.killbill.io/latest/aviate-authentication.html[Aviate Authentication].

A request outside the member's role is refused with `403 Forbidden`.
