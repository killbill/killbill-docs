=== Overview

The Deposit UI is only available after the https://github.com/killbill/killbill-deposit-plugin[Deposit Plugin] has been deployed.

The UI allows you to record offline payments, such as wire transfers or checks, against the invoices of an account via Kaui.

=== Accessing the UI

The Deposit UI can be accessed by clicking the "Create Deposit" button in the "Billing Information" section of the account page.

image::../assets/img/kaui/deposit-create-button.png[width=850,align="center"]

This displays the "Record Payment" UI:

image::../assets/img/kaui/deposit-record-payment.png[width=850,align="center"]

=== Recording a Deposit

The Deposit UI allows recording a deposit against one or more unpaid invoices of the account.

1. Open the Deposit UI as explained above. The "Account ID" field is pre-populated with the current account, and the "Invoices" section lists the account invoices along with their balance.
2. Enter the following details:
* **Amount Received:** The total amount received. The currency of the account is displayed below this field.
* **Payment Date:** The date on which the payment was received.
* **Payment Ref. #:** The reference of the payment, for example the wire or check number.
* **Payment Mode:** The mode of the payment, for example `Wire`.
3. In the "Invoices" section, enter the amount to be applied to each invoice in the "Payment" column.
4. Click on the "Save" button.

Kill Bill records a payment for each invoice with a non-zero payment amount, and reduces the invoice balance accordingly. The payments are listed in the "Payments" tab of the account.

NOTE: The payment amount for an invoice cannot be greater than the invoice balance. Refer to the https://docs.killbill.io/latest/deposit-plugin[Deposit Plugin] documentation for more details.
