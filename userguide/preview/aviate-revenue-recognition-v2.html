Revenue Recognition

The Aviate Revenue Recognition module turns the invoices that Kill Bill produces into revenue schedules. It spreads each invoiced amount over the period in which the service is delivered and tracks, day by day, how much of that amount has been recognized as revenue and how much is still deferred. Finance teams use it to see deferred and recognized revenue per accounting month, and to close and reopen those months.

This guide describes the functionality that is available today, the rules it follows, and how to use it through the Aviate API.

Overview

When you bill a customer in advance, for example a monthly or annual subscription, the cash and the invoice arrive before the service is delivered. Accounting practice generally treats that amount as deferred revenue that becomes recognized revenue over the service period.

The Revenue Recognition module automates this for invoices generated by Kill Bill:

  • It creates a revenue schedule for each eligible invoice item as soon as the invoice is generated.

  • It splits the schedule into dated revenue entries, either one per day of the service period or a single entry for one-time charges.

  • A background process marks entries as recognized once their date is reached.

  • Every entry belongs to an accounting period (a calendar month), which you can close at month-end and reopen if needed.

  • Reports show recognized and deferred revenue by period, by currency, by plan and by recognition method.

The module answers questions such as:

  • How much of what we invoiced has been earned so far, and how much is still deferred?

  • How much revenue was recognized in March?

  • Which schedules does a given account or subscription have, and where does each one stand?

Note: The module calculates and reports revenue from billing data. It does not post entries to a general ledger and does not export journals. Whether its output meets a particular accounting standard is for your finance team to determine.

Key Features

| Feature | Description | |---------|-------------| | Automatic revenue schedules | A schedule is created for every eligible invoice item when the invoice is generated. No manual setup is required. | | Two recognition methods | Straight-line (daily) for recurring and usage charges; immediate for fixed (one-time) and external charges. | | Daily recognition | A background process recognizes all entries whose date has been reached. By default it runs once a day. | | Monthly accounting periods | Periods named YYYY-MM are created automatically as revenue entries are generated. You can also create periods yourself. | | Period close and reopen | Close a month once all of its revenue has been recognized; reopen it if you need to. | | Adjustment handling | Invoice item adjustments, cancellation credits and standalone account credits are reflected in the affected schedules. | | Schedule lookup | Look up schedules by invoice item or schedule ID, or list them with filters such as account, subscription, plan, currency and status. | | Revenue reports | Period summary, per-currency period summary, revenue waterfall, revenue by plan and recognition rate. |

Before you begin

Revenue recognition is disabled by default. Your Aviate administrator enables it in the plugin configuration:

| Property | Default | Description | |----------|---------|-------------| | com.killbill.billing.plugin.aviate.enableRevenueRecognition | false | Set to true to create revenue schedules for new invoices and to start the daily recognition process. | | com.killbill.billing.plugin.aviate.revenueRecognition.delaySeconds | 86400 (one day) | How often the recognition process runs, in seconds. |

Revenue schedules are created as invoices are generated. Enable the feature before you start billing the customers whose revenue you want to track.

Accounting Periods

An accounting period groups revenue entries by calendar month. Periods are identified by name, in the format YYYY-MM (for example 2026-04).

How periods are created

  • Automatically. When Aviate creates revenue entries for an invoice item, it creates any monthly period that does not already exist, from the first to the last day of that month.

  • Manually. You can create a period ahead of time by giving it a name, a start date and an end date.

Every revenue entry is assigned to the period named after the month of its date. For example, an entry dated 2026-04-17 belongs to period 2026-04. When you create a period manually, use the same YYYY-MM name with the first and last day of the month. A period with any other name receives no revenue entries.

Supported operations

| Operation | What it does | |-----------|--------------| | Create | Creates a period with a name, start date and end date. The name must be unique, and the end date cannot be before the start date. | | List | Lists all periods. Can be filtered to only open or only closed periods. | | View | Shows one period: its dates, whether it is closed, and when and by whom it was closed. | | Close | Closes an open period. Rejected if the period still contains unrecognized revenue entries, unless you force the close. | | Reopen | Reopens a closed period. Its unrecognized entries become eligible for recognition again. | | Summary | Shows recognized and deferred revenue and the number of entries in the period, for all currencies combined or per currency. |

Closing a period

To close a period:

  • The period must exist and must not already be closed.

  • All revenue entries dated in that period must already be recognized. Otherwise the close is rejected with a message like Cannot close period 2026-04: 12 unrecognized entries remain.

  • You can override this check with the force option. Use it when you have reviewed the remaining entries and still want to close the month. For a common case where this is needed, see [Cancellations and adjustments](#cancellations-and-adjustments).

When a period is closed:

  • The date and the user who closed it are recorded.

  • The daily recognition process no longer recognizes any remaining unrecognized entries in that period.

  • Aviate does not create revenue entries for new invoice items whose service dates fall in that period (see [Closed periods](#closed-periods)).

Reopening a period

Only a closed period can be reopened. Reopening clears the closed date and closed-by user. Entries in the period that were not yet recognized become eligible again, and the next recognition run can pick them up. Entries that were already recognized are not changed.

Revenue Recognition Process

1. Schedule creation (when an invoice is generated)

When Kill Bill generates an invoice, Aviate examines each invoice item and creates one revenue schedule per eligible item:

| Invoice item type | Recognition method | How the amount is spread | |-------------------|--------------------|--------------------------| | Recurring | Straight-line | Evenly, one entry per day of the service period | | Usage | Straight-line | Evenly, one entry per day of the service period | | Fixed (one-time) | Immediate | One entry for the full amount on the service start date | | External charge | Immediate | One entry for the full amount on the service start date |

Items with a zero or negative amount, and other item types such as taxes and credits, do not get a schedule. Each invoice item has at most one schedule.

Each schedule records:

  • The invoice, invoice item, account, subscription, plan and phase it came from.

  • Its currency and total amount.

  • Its service period and recognition method.

  • Its running recognized and deferred amounts. At creation, the full amount is deferred.

  • Whether it is closed, meaning fully recognized or ended early by an adjustment.

2. Straight-line calculation

For straight-line schedules:

  • The service period runs from the item’s start date up to, but not including, its end date. A 1 January to 1 February period is 31 days.

  • The daily amount is the total divided by the number of days, rounded to nine decimal places.

  • The last day takes any rounding difference, so the entries always add up exactly to the schedule total.

3. Daily recognition

A background process runs at the configured interval, once a day by default. Each run:

  • Recognizes every unrecognized entry dated on or before the current date (UTC) in an open period, including entries dated today.

  • Updates each affected schedule’s recognized and deferred amounts.

  • Closes a schedule once its full amount has been recognized.

Recognizing revenue for a specific month

There is no customer-facing operation that runs recognition on demand for a chosen date or month. Revenue for a month is recognized by the regular runs as its days pass. All of a month’s entries have been processed once a run has taken place on or after the last day of that month.

The month-end workflow is:

  1. Wait for a recognition run on or after the last day of the month.

  2. Review the month with the period summary.

  3. Close the period.

See [Close a month](#close-a-month) for the steps.

Business Rules and Constraints

Recognition rules

  • The recognition method depends only on the invoice item type. It cannot be configured per plan, product or customer.

  • Recognition follows the service dates on the invoice item, not the invoice date or the payment date. Revenue is recognized whether or not the invoice has been paid.

  • Revenue is always assigned to calendar months. Custom fiscal calendars (for example 4-4-5 periods) are not supported.

Closed periods

  • Closing a period does not block billing. Invoices, credits, adjustments and payments dated in a closed period are still accepted by Kill Bill.

  • If a new invoice item has service dates in a closed period, Aviate does not create its revenue entries. If you expect back-dated invoices, reopen the affected period before the invoice is generated.

  • Adjustments and credits processed after a period is closed still update the affected schedules (see below).

Cancellations and adjustments

When an invoice item is reduced after its schedule exists, for example by an item adjustment or by the credit Kill Bill issues when a subscription is cancelled or changed mid-period:

  • If the item still has deferred revenue, Aviate records a reversal entry for the adjustment amount, ends the schedule at the adjustment date and closes it. Days after that date are not recognized.

  • If the item’s revenue has already been fully recognized, the schedule is not changed and no reversal is recorded.

  • The days that were not recognized remain in their month as unrecognized entries. Closing a month that contains a mid-period cancellation or adjustment therefore requires the force option.

  • Reversal entries are not assigned to an accounting period, so they do not appear in period summaries or in a waterfall that has a start period.

Account credits

  • A credit created on its own, outside an invoice that also contains charges, reduces the deferred revenue of the account’s open schedules. You cannot choose which schedules a credit is applied to.

  • Credits that appear on the same invoice as recurring, usage, fixed or external charges are not treated as revenue adjustments and do not change any schedule.

Reports

Reports are built from two different sources. Their figures can differ after cancellations, adjustments or credits.

| Report | Built from | Currency handling | |--------|-----------|-------------------| | Period summary | Revenue entries in the period | All currencies added together | | Period currency summary | Revenue entries in the period | One row per currency | | Revenue waterfall | Revenue entries, by period | One row per period and currency | | Revenue by plan | Schedule totals | All currencies added together | | Recognition rate | Schedule totals, by recognition method | All currencies added together |

  • In entry-based reports, "deferred" is the total of entries not yet recognized. This includes entries left unrecognized after a cancellation or adjustment.

  • For tenants that bill in more than one currency, use the period currency summary or the waterfall to avoid adding amounts in different currencies together.

Access and permissions

  • All revenue operations apply to the tenant identified by the Kill Bill API key and secret on the request.

  • When role-based access control is enabled in Aviate, any authenticated role can read revenue data. Creating, closing and reopening periods requires one of the roles finance_admin, finance_vp, system_admin, admin or super_admin.

Data you cannot edit

Revenue schedules and entries are derived from invoices. You cannot create, edit or delete them directly. To change revenue, change the underlying invoice, for example with an item adjustment.

Usage Instructions

All operations are available through the Aviate REST API under:

http://<killbill-host>:8080/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue

Every request needs:

  • Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN} (your Aviate token)

  • X-killbill-apiKey and X-killbill-apisecret (your tenant credentials)

Parameters are passed in the query string. As with other Aviate actions, send POST requests with Content-Type: application/json and an empty JSON body ({}).

The examples below use these shell variables:

export AVIATE=http://127.0.0.1:8080/plugins/aviate-plugin
export API_KEY=<your-api-key>
export API_SECRET=<your-api-secret>

Look up revenue schedules

List schedules with any combination of filters:

| Query parameter | Description | |-----------------|-------------| | accountId | Schedules of one account | | subscriptionId | Schedules of one subscription | | planName | Schedules for one plan | | currency | Schedules in one currency, for example USD | | closed | true for closed schedules, false for open ones | | servicePeriodStart | Schedules whose service period starts on or after this date (YYYY-MM-DD) | | offset, limit | Paging. limit defaults to and cannot exceed 1000. |

Results are returned newest first.

curl -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/schedules?accountId=${ACCOUNT_ID}&closed=false&limit=50" | jq .

Get the schedule of an invoice item, using the Kill Bill invoice item ID:

curl -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/${INVOICE_ITEM_ID}/schedule" | jq .

Get a schedule by its ID. Schedule IDs are numbers and are returned in the schedule details.

curl -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/schedules/${SCHEDULE_ID}" | jq .

List the revenue entries of a schedule, showing each entry’s date, amount, period and whether it has been recognized:

curl -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/${SCHEDULE_ID}/entriesPerSchedule" | jq .

Manage accounting periods

Create a period. All three parameters are required. A successful request returns 201 Created.

curl -X POST \
     -H'Content-Type: application/json' \
     -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     -d '{}' \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/periods?periodName=2026-05&startDate=2026-05-01&endDate=2026-05-31"

List periods. Add closed=true or closed=false to filter.

curl -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/periods?closed=false" | jq .

View one period:

curl -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/periods/2026-04" | jq .

Close a month

  1. Check that the month has been processed. A recognition run must have taken place on or after the last day of the month.

  2. Review the period summary. It returns the period name, the recognized amount, the deferred (not yet recognized) amount and the number of entries.

    ```
curl -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/periods/2026-04/summary" | jq .
```
    For figures per currency, use `currencySummary` instead:
    ```
curl -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/periods/2026-04/currencySummary" | jq .
```

  3. Close the period. A successful close returns 204 No Content.

    ```
curl -X POST \
     -H'Content-Type: application/json' \
     -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     -d '{}' \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/periods/2026-04/close"
```

  4. If the close is rejected because unrecognized entries remain, look at the schedules involved (for example, those closed early by a cancellation). If you still want to close the month, repeat the request with force=true:

    ```
curl -X POST \
     -H'Content-Type: application/json' \
     -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     -d '{}' \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/periods/2026-04/close?force=true"
```

Reopen a month

A successful reopen returns 204 No Content. The request is rejected if the period does not exist or is not closed.

curl -X POST \
     -H'Content-Type: application/json' \
     -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     -d '{}' \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/periods/2026-04/reopen"

Run reports

Revenue waterfall. Recognized and deferred revenue per period and currency. All parameters are optional: startPeriod and endPeriod (YYYY-MM, inclusive) and currency.

curl -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/reports/waterfall?startPeriod=2026-01&endPeriod=2026-06&currency=USD" | jq .

Revenue by plan. Total, recognized and deferred amounts and the number of schedules per plan, across the whole tenant. Takes no parameters.

curl -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/reports/by-plan" | jq .

Recognition rate. For each recognition method (straight-line or immediate): total amount, recognized amount, the share recognized so far and the number of schedules, across the whole tenant. Takes no parameters.

curl -H"Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
     -H"X-killbill-apisecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
     "${AVIATE}/v1/revenue/reports/recognition-rate" | jq .

Examples

Example 1: Monthly subscription

A customer is invoiced USD 31.00 for a monthly plan covering 2026-01-01 to 2026-02-01.

  • Aviate creates a straight-line schedule with a total of 31.00, all deferred.

  • The service period is 31 days, so Aviate creates 31 entries of 1.00, dated 2026-01-01 to 2026-01-31, all in period 2026-01.

  • Each daily run recognizes the entries dated up to that day. After the run on 2026-01-10, 10.00 is recognized and 21.00 is deferred.

  • After the run on 2026-01-31, the full 31.00 is recognized and the schedule is closed.

Example 2: Quarterly subscription spanning three months

A customer is invoiced USD 90.00 for a quarterly plan covering 2026-01-01 to 2026-04-01, which is 90 days. The daily amount is 1.00, so the revenue falls into three periods:

| Period | Days | Revenue | |--------|------|---------| | 2026-01 | 31 | 31.00 | | 2026-02 | 28 | 28.00 | | 2026-03 | 31 | 31.00 | | Total | 90 | 90.00 |

At the end of January, 31.00 is recognized and 59.00 is still deferred.

Example 3: Rounding

A customer is invoiced USD 100.00 for 2026-04-01 to 2026-05-01 (30 days).

  • Each of the first 29 entries is 3.333333333 (100 ÷ 30, rounded to nine decimals).

  • The last entry, on 2026-04-30, is 3.333333343, so that the 30 entries add up to exactly 100.00.

Example 4: One-time setup fee

A customer is invoiced a fixed USD 250.00 setup fee with a service start date of 2026-04-15.

  • Aviate creates an immediate schedule with one entry of 250.00 dated 2026-04-15, in period 2026-04.

  • The first recognition run on or after 2026-04-15 recognizes the full amount and closes the schedule.

Example 5: Month-end close

On 2026-05-02, the finance team closes April:

  1. The daily run on 2026-04-30 or later has recognized all April entries.

  2. GET /v1/revenue/periods/2026-04/summary shows the recognized total for April with a deferred amount of 0.

  3. POST /v1/revenue/periods/2026-04/close returns 204 No Content.

  4. GET /v1/revenue/periods?closed=true now lists 2026-04 with the closing date and user.

If a subscription had been cancelled in the middle of April, its remaining April days would still be unrecognized. Step 3 would be rejected with Cannot close period 2026-04: N unrecognized entries remain. After review, the team would close the month with force=true.

Example 6: Mid-period cancellation

A USD 31.00 schedule covers 2026-01-01 to 2026-02-01. By 2026-01-15, 15.00 has been recognized. The subscription is then cancelled with effect from 2026-01-16, and Kill Bill credits the unused 16.00.

  • Aviate records a reversal entry of −16.00, ends the schedule on 2026-01-16 and closes it.

  • Days after 2026-01-16 are not recognized.

  • The unrecognized January days stay in period 2026-01. Closing January therefore requires force=true.

  • The reversal entry has no accounting period, so it does not appear in the January period summary.
