== Introduction

The Aviate Revenue Recognition module provides tools for managing when and how revenue is recognized from your invoices. It gives finance teams visibility into recognized vs. deferred revenue through managed accounting periods.

Your accounting policy remains the responsibility of your finance team. Using this module does not by itself make your accounting compliant with a revenue standard such as ASC 606 or IFRS 15.

Key capabilities include:

* *Accounting periods* -- monthly periods that can be opened, closed, and reported on
* *Revenue schedules* -- automatic recognition of revenue across the service period of each invoice item
* *Contract-aware allocation* -- revenue allocated across multiple performance obligations using relative standalone selling prices
* *Waterfall reports* -- period-by-period view of recognized and deferred revenue

== Key Concepts

=== Accounting Periods

An accounting period represents a calendar month during which revenue recognition entries are recorded. A period is in one of two states:

* *OPEN*: revenue can be recognized in the period
* *CLOSED*: normal recognition into the period stops

Reopening a closed period sets it back to OPEN.

Periods are auto-created monthly (named `2026-01`, `2026-02`, etc.) as invoices are generated. They can be closed at month-end.

=== Revenue Recognition Schedules

Aviate creates one revenue schedule for each recurring, usage, fixed or external charge invoice item with an amount above zero. Other invoice items, such as tax and credits, get no schedule.

The recognition method depends on the type of invoice item. In this release it cannot be set per plan or per product.

[cols="1,1,2", options="header"]
|===
| Invoice item type | Method | What happens

| Recurring
| Straight line
| The amount is spread evenly over each day of the service period

| Usage
| Straight line
| The amount is spread evenly over each day of the usage period on the invoice

| Fixed (for example a setup fee)
| Immediate
| The full amount is recognized on the service start date

| External charge
| Immediate
| The full amount is recognized on the service start date
|===

The service period of an invoice item ends the day before its end date. A period from January 1 to February 1 covers the 31 days of January.

Straight-line revenue is calculated per day, then added up per month. Monthly amounts therefore follow the number of days in each month. The last day takes any rounding difference, so the daily amounts add up exactly to the schedule amount. When a contract allocation applies (see <<Contract Revenue Allocation>>), the schedule amount is the allocated amount.

For example, an annual subscription of $1,200.00 for the period from January 1, 2026 to January 1, 2027 (365 days) gives about $3.29 per day:

[cols="2,1,1", options="header"]
|===
| Month | Days | Recognized

| January 2026
| 31
| $101.92

| February 2026
| 28
| $92.05

| April 2026
| 30
| $98.63

| Full year
| 365
| $1,200.00
|===

Monthly amounts are rounded to the cent, so the sum of the twelve rounded months can differ from the total by one cent.

=== When Revenue Is Recognized

Creating a schedule does not recognize revenue. A recognition job runs about every 24 hours by default. Each run recognizes the scheduled amounts dated on or before the current date (UTC). A month is fully recognized after the first run on or after its last day.

Scheduled revenue belongs to the accounting period of its service date, not to the date the job ran. Revenue for April recognized by a run in May stays in April.

=== Contract Revenue Allocation (Relative SSP)

When a contract includes multiple performance obligations (e.g., a subscription + setup + support), revenue is allocated proportionally based on *standalone selling prices* (SSP):

. Identify the performance obligations in the contract
. Determine the standalone selling price (SSP) for each obligation
. Allocate the total transaction price proportionally
. Recognize each allocated amount with the method of its invoice item

== Getting Started with Revenue Recognition

=== Installing the Plugin

Install the Aviate plugin as described in the https://docs.killbill.io/latest/how-to-install-the-aviate-plugin.html[How to Install the Aviate Plugin] guide.

=== Enabling Revenue Recognition

To enable revenue recognition, start Kill Bill with the following system property:

[source,bash]
----
com.killbill.billing.plugin.aviate.enableRevenueRecognition=true
----

For details on setting configuration properties, refer to the https://docs.killbill.io/latest/userguide_configuration.html[Kill Bill Configuration Guide].

=== Using Revenue Recognition APIs

Full API documentation is available at https://apidocs.killbill.io/aviate-revrec[our API documentation].

== Managing Accounting Periods

=== Listing Periods

[source,bash]
----
curl "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/periods" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

The response lists each period with its name, its start and end dates, and whether it is closed. Add `?closed=true` or `?closed=false` to filter.

=== Closing a Period

[source,bash]
----
curl -X POST "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/periods/2026-01/close" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

If entries in the period are still unrecognized, the close is rejected and the response gives the number of unrecognized entries.

A typical month-end close:

. Wait until the recognition job has run on or after the last day of the month.
. Check the period summary and the waterfall report for that month.
. Close the period. If the close is still rejected after the job has run, contact Kill Bill support.

Closing a period does not block new invoices, credits or adjustments for that month. See <<Invoice Adjustments, Credits and Cancellations>>.

=== Reopening a Period

A closed period can be reopened:

[source,bash]
----
curl -X POST "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/periods/2026-01/reopen" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

[NOTE]
*Note:* In this preview, reopening a period does not keep a record of the earlier close. To review a closed month again, reopen it, check it after the next recognition run, then close it again.

== Revenue Waterfall Report

The waterfall report shows recognized and deferred revenue per accounting period and currency:

[source,bash]
----
curl "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/reports/waterfall?startPeriod=2026-01&endPeriod=2026-06&currency=USD" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

All three query parameters are optional. The report is returned as JSON, so you can load it into a spreadsheet or another reporting tool.

== Contract Revenue Allocation

When contracts have multiple performance obligations, Aviate uses standalone selling prices (SSP) to allocate revenue.

=== How Allocation Works

Consider a contract with three components:

* Enterprise subscription: $10,000/year (SSP: $10,000)
* Implementation services: $3,000 one-time (SSP: $5,000)
* Premium support: $2,000/year (SSP: $2,000)
* *Total contract price*: $15,000

Using relative SSP allocation:

[cols="1,1,1,1", options="header"]
|===
| Obligation | SSP | SSP % | Allocated Revenue

| Subscription
| $10,000
| 58.8%
| $8,824

| Implementation
| $5,000
| 29.4%
| $4,412

| Support
| $2,000
| 11.8%
| $1,765
|===

Note that the implementation fee ($3,000 charged) receives an allocation of $4,412 because the SSP-based allocation distributes the total contract price proportionally.

The allocation is computed when the first invoice for the contract is generated. Later SSP changes do not change it.

=== Setting Standalone Selling Prices

Standalone selling prices are set per plan, phase and currency, with a validity period. You can manage them on the SSP screen in Aviate, or through the API (`POST ${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/ssp`). See the https://apidocs.killbill.io/aviate-revrec[API documentation] for the parameters.

We recommend setting explicit SSPs for every plan used in a contract to ensure consistent and auditable allocations.

== Viewing Revenue Details

=== By Account

[source,bash]
----
curl "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/accounts/a1b2c3d4-..." \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

=== By Contract

Contract allocations and the revenue schedules of a contract are available through two calls. The contract ID is a UUID.

[source,bash]
----
curl "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/contracts/${CONTRACT_ID}/allocations" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"

curl "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/contracts/${CONTRACT_ID}/schedules" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

== Invoice Adjustments, Credits and Cancellations

Adjustments, credits and cancellations can change revenue schedules. How these changes affect recognized and deferred revenue, and how they interact with closed periods, is still being finalized in this preview. During the preview, review revenue for any month that contains an adjustment, a credit or a mid-period cancellation before you close it. If you are testing this module, tell us which treatment your accounting policy requires.

== Not Included in This Preview

* Accounting journal entries, or an export to an ERP or general ledger
* A recognition method set per plan or per product
* Equal monthly amounts with prorated first and last months. Straight-line revenue is calculated per day
* Editing or reversing a revenue entry by hand. Schedules are created from invoices
* Approval of a schedule before recognition
* Blocking invoices, credits or adjustments in a closed period
