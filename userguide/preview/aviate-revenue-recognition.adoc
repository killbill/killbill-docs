= Aviate Revenue Recognition

include::{sourcedir}/includes/insider-preview-card.adoc[]

== Introduction

The Aviate Revenue Recognition module manages when revenue is recognized from the invoices that Kill Bill generates. It spreads each invoiced amount over the period in which the service is delivered, and tracks how much of it has been recognized and how much is still deferred. Finance teams get visibility into recognized vs. deferred revenue through monthly accounting periods that they can close and reopen.

The module applies concepts from ASC 606 (Revenue from Contracts with Customers): revenue is recognized as the service is delivered. Aviate calculates and reports revenue from billing data. It does not post entries to a general ledger or export journals. Your finance team remains responsible for confirming that the resulting treatment meets the accounting standards that apply to you.

Key capabilities include:

* *Automatic revenue schedules* -- every eligible invoice item gets a schedule that spreads its amount over the item's service period
* *Daily recognition* -- a background process recognizes revenue as each day of service is delivered
* *Accounting periods* -- monthly periods that are created automatically and can be closed, reopened and reported on
* *Reports* -- period summaries, a period-by-period waterfall of recognized and deferred revenue, revenue by plan and recognition rate
* *Schedule lookups* -- schedules and their daily entries by invoice item, account, subscription, plan, currency or contract

== Key Concepts

=== Revenue Schedules and Entries

When Kill Bill generates an invoice, Aviate creates one *revenue schedule* for each eligible invoice item. The schedule records the invoice, invoice item, account, subscription, plan and phase it came from, together with its currency, total amount, service period and recognition method. It also keeps running *recognized* and *deferred* amounts. At creation, the full amount is deferred.

Each schedule is split into dated *revenue entries*. An entry is recognized once its date is reached. When the full amount of a schedule has been recognized, the schedule is closed.

[[revrec-recognition-methods]]
=== Recognition Methods

The recognition method depends only on the type of the invoice item. It cannot be configured per plan, product or customer.

[cols="1,1,2", options="header"]
|===
| Invoice item type | Recognition method | How the amount is spread

| Recurring
| Straight-line
| Evenly, one entry per day of the service period

| Usage
| Straight-line
| Evenly, one entry per day of the item's service period

| Fixed (one-time)
| Immediate
| One entry for the full amount on the service start date

| External charge
| Immediate
| One entry for the full amount on the service start date
|===

Items with a zero or negative amount, and other item types such as taxes and credits, do not get a schedule. Each invoice item has at most one schedule.

For straight-line schedules:

* The service period runs from the item's start date up to, but *not including*, its end date. A 1 January to 1 February period is 31 days.
* The daily amount is the total divided by the number of days, rounded to nine decimal places.
* The last day takes any rounding difference, so the entries always add up exactly to the schedule total.

For example:

* A $1,200 annual subscription billed for 1 January 2026 to 1 January 2027 is spread over 365 days, at about $3.29 a day. January (31 days) receives $101.92, February (28 days) $92.05 and April (30 days) $98.63.
* A $500 one-time setup fee is recognized in full on its service start date.

=== Accounting Periods

An accounting period groups revenue entries by calendar month. Periods are identified by name, in the format `YYYY-MM` (for example `2026-04`). Every revenue entry belongs to the period named after the month of its date. For example, an entry dated `2026-04-17` belongs to period `2026-04`.

A period is either *open* or *closed*:

* *Open* -- the daily recognition process recognizes the period's entries as their dates are reached.
* *Closed* -- the daily recognition process no longer recognizes the period's remaining entries, and the date and user that closed it are recorded. A closed period can be reopened, which makes it open again.

Periods are created automatically, one per calendar month, as invoices are generated. You can also create a period ahead of time. Close a period at month-end once its revenue has been recognized.

[NOTE]
*Note:* Closing a period does not block billing. Kill Bill still accepts invoices, credits, adjustments and payments dated in a closed period, and adjustments still update the affected revenue schedules. See <<revrec-business-rules>>.

== Getting Started with Revenue Recognition

=== Installing the Plugin

Install the Aviate plugin as described in the https://docs.killbill.io/latest/how-to-install-the-aviate-plugin.html[How to Install the Aviate Plugin] guide.

=== Enabling Revenue Recognition

Revenue recognition is disabled by default. To enable it, start Kill Bill with the following system property:

[source,bash]
----
com.killbill.billing.plugin.aviate.enableRevenueRecognition=true
----

The following properties control revenue recognition:

[cols="2,1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Property | Default | Description

| `com.killbill.billing.plugin.aviate.enableRevenueRecognition`
| `false`
| Set to `true` to create revenue schedules for new invoices and to start the daily recognition process.

| `com.killbill.billing.plugin.aviate.revenueRecognition.delaySeconds`
| `86400` (one day)
| How often the recognition process runs, in seconds.
|===

Revenue schedules are created as invoices are generated. Enable the feature before you start billing the customers whose revenue you want to track.

For details on setting configuration properties, refer to the https://docs.killbill.io/latest/userguide_configuration.html[Kill Bill Configuration Guide].

=== Using Revenue Recognition APIs

Full API documentation is available at https://apidocs.killbill.io/aviate-revrec[our API documentation].

All revenue recognition endpoints are under `${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue`. Every request needs your Aviate token (`Authorization: Bearer ...`) and your tenant's Kill Bill API key and secret. Parameters are passed in the query string. Send `POST` requests with `Content-Type: application/json` and an empty JSON body (`{}`).


== How Revenue Is Recognized

=== Schedule Creation

When Kill Bill generates an invoice, Aviate creates a revenue schedule for each eligible invoice item and generates its entries (see <<revrec-recognition-methods>>). It also creates the monthly accounting periods those entries fall into, if they do not exist yet.

=== Daily Recognition

A background process runs at the configured interval, once a day by default. Each run:

* Recognizes every unrecognized entry dated *on or before the current date (UTC)* in an open period, including entries dated today.
* Updates each affected schedule's recognized and deferred amounts.
* Closes a schedule once its full amount has been recognized.

=== Recognizing Revenue for a Specific Month

In production, revenue for a month is recognized by the regular runs as its days pass. All of a month's entries have been processed once a run has taken place on or after the last day of that month.

The month-end workflow is:

. Wait for a recognition run on or after the last day of the month.
. Review the month with the period summary.
. Close the period.

See <<revrec-closing-period>> for the steps.

==== Running Recognition On Demand

The `triggerRevenueRecognition` endpoint runs recognition immediately, up to a date that you choose:

[source,bash]
----
curl -X POST "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/triggerRevenueRecognition?recognitionDate=2026-04-30" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{}'
----

[cols="1,1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Parameter | Required | Description

| `recognitionDate`
| Yes
| Recognize entries dated on or before this date (`YYYY-MM-DD`)
|===

A successful request returns `204 No Content`. A missing `recognitionDate` is rejected with `recognitionDate is required`.

The run works like a daily run, except that it uses `recognitionDate` instead of the current date:

* It recognizes every unrecognized entry dated on or before `recognitionDate` in an open period, and updates and closes schedules in the same way.
* It applies to *all accounts of the tenant*. It cannot be limited to an account, a contract or a single month.
* A `recognitionDate` in the future recognizes revenue for service that has not been delivered yet.
* Recognized entries cannot be reverted. Reopening a period does not change entries that were already recognized.
* Do not run it at the same time as the scheduled recognition run.

== Managing Accounting Periods

=== Creating a Period

Periods are created automatically, but you can create one ahead of time. All three parameters are required. The name must be unique, and the end date cannot be before the start date. A successful request returns `201 Created`.

[source,bash]
----
curl -X POST "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/periods?periodName=2026-05&startDate=2026-05-01&endDate=2026-05-31" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{}'
----

[NOTE]
*Note:* Revenue entries are assigned to periods by name. Use the `YYYY-MM` format with the first and last day of the month. A period with any other name receives no revenue entries.

=== Listing Periods

Add `closed=true` or `closed=false` to list only closed or only open periods.

[source,bash]
----
curl "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/periods?closed=false" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

To view a single period, use `GET /v1/revenue/periods/{name}`, for example `/v1/revenue/periods/2026-04`.

Each period includes:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Field | Description

| `periodName`
| The period name, for example `2026-04`

| `startDate`, `endDate`
| The first and last day of the period

| `closed`
| `true` if the period is closed

| `closedDate`, `closedBy`
| When and by whom the period was closed. Empty for open periods.
|===

=== Viewing a Period Summary

The period summary returns the recognized amount, the deferred (not yet recognized) amount and the number of entries in the period, for all currencies combined:

[source,bash]
----
curl "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/periods/2026-04/summary" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

For one row per currency, use `currencySummary` instead:

[source,bash]
----
curl "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/periods/2026-04/currencySummary" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

If you bill in more than one currency, use the currency summary. The plain summary adds amounts in different currencies together.

[[revrec-closing-period]]
=== Closing a Period

To close a month:

. Check that a recognition run has taken place on or after the last day of the month.
. Review the period summary.
. Close the period. A successful close returns `204 No Content`.

[source,bash]
----
curl -X POST "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/periods/2026-01/close" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{}'
----

The close is rejected if the period does not exist, is already closed, or still contains unrecognized revenue entries. In the last case the error reports how many entries remain, for example `Cannot close period 2026-01: 12 unrecognized entries remain`.

After reviewing the remaining entries, you can override this check with `force=true`:

[source,bash]
----
curl -X POST "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/periods/2026-01/close?force=true" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{}'
----

A month that contains a mid-period cancellation or adjustment can only be closed with `force=true` (see <<revrec-cancellations>>).

=== Reopening a Period

In exceptional cases, a closed period can be reopened for corrections. A successful reopen returns `204 No Content`. The request is rejected if the period does not exist or is not closed.

[source,bash]
----
curl -X POST "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/periods/2026-01/reopen" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{}'
----

Reopening clears the closed date and closed-by user. Entries in the period that were not yet recognized become eligible again, and the next recognition run can pick them up. Entries that were already recognized are not changed.

== Revenue Reports

=== Revenue Waterfall

The waterfall report shows recognized and deferred revenue for each accounting period, with one row per period and currency:

[source,bash]
----
curl "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/reports/waterfall?startPeriod=2026-01&endPeriod=2026-06&currency=USD" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

All parameters are optional:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Parameter | Description

| `startPeriod`
| First period to include (`YYYY-MM`, inclusive)

| `endPeriod`
| Last period to include (`YYYY-MM`, inclusive)

| `currency`
| Only include revenue in this currency, for example `USD`
|===

Each row contains the accounting period, the currency, the recognized amount, the deferred (not yet recognized) amount and the number of entries.

=== Revenue by Plan

Total, recognized and deferred amounts and the number of schedules for each plan, across the whole tenant. Takes no parameters.

[source,bash]
----
curl "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/reports/by-plan" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

=== Recognition Rate

For each recognition method (straight-line or immediate): total amount, recognized amount, the share recognized so far and the number of schedules, across the whole tenant. Takes no parameters.

[source,bash]
----
curl "${KB_URL}/plugins/aviate-plugin/v1/revenue/reports/recognition-rate" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer ${ID_TOKEN}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiKey: ${API_KEY}" \
  -H "X-Killbill-ApiSecret: ${API_SECRET}"
----

=== How Reports Are Calculated

Reports are built from two different sources. Their figures can differ after cancellations, adjustments or credits.

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Report | Built from | Currency handling

| Period summary
| Revenue entries in the period
| All currencies added together

| Period currency summary
| Revenue entries in the period
| One row per currency

| Revenue waterfall
| Revenue entries, by period
| One row per period and currency

| Revenue by plan
| Schedule totals
| All currencies added together

| Recognition rate
| Schedule totals, by recognition method
| All currencies added together
|===

In entry-based reports, "deferred" is the total of entries *not yet recognized*. This includes entries left unrecognized after a cancellation or adjustment.

[[revrec-business-rules]]
== Business Rules and Constraints

=== Recognition

* Recognition follows the service dates on the invoice item, not the invoice date or the payment date. Revenue is recognized whether or not the invoice has been paid.
* Revenue is always assigned to calendar months. Custom fiscal calendars (for example 4-4-5 periods) are not supported.

=== Closed Periods

* Closing a period does not block billing. Kill Bill still accepts invoices, credits, adjustments and payments dated in a closed period.
* If a new invoice item has service dates in a closed period, Aviate does not create its revenue entries. If you expect back-dated invoices, reopen the affected period *before* the invoice is generated.
* Adjustments and credits processed after a period is closed still update the affected schedules.

[[revrec-cancellations]]
=== Cancellations and Adjustments

When an invoice item is reduced after its schedule exists, for example by an item adjustment or by the credit Kill Bill issues when a subscription is cancelled or changed mid-period:

* If the item still has deferred revenue, Aviate records a *reversal entry* for the adjustment amount, ends the schedule at the adjustment date and closes it. Days after that date are not recognized.
* If the item's revenue has already been fully recognized, the schedule is not changed and no reversal is recorded.
* The days that were not recognized remain in their month as unrecognized entries. Closing a month that contains a mid-period cancellation or adjustment therefore requires `force=true`.
* Reversal entries are not assigned to an accounting period, so they do not appear in period summaries or in a waterfall that has a start period.

=== Account Credits

* A credit created on its own, outside an invoice that also contains charges, reduces the deferred revenue of the account's open schedules. You cannot choose which schedules a credit is applied to.
* Credits that appear on the same invoice as recurring, usage, fixed or external charges are not treated as revenue adjustments and do not change any schedule.

=== Data You Cannot Edit

Revenue schedules and entries are derived from invoices. You cannot create, edit or delete them directly. To change revenue, change the underlying invoice, for example with an item adjustment.

== Examples

=== Monthly Subscription

A customer is invoiced *$31.00* for a monthly plan covering `2026-01-01` to `2026-02-01`.

* Aviate creates a straight-line schedule with a total of $31.00, all deferred.
* The service period is 31 days, so Aviate creates 31 entries of $1.00, dated `2026-01-01` to `2026-01-31`, all in period `2026-01`.
* After the run on `2026-01-10`, $10.00 is recognized and $21.00 is deferred.
* After the run on `2026-01-31`, the full $31.00 is recognized and the schedule is closed.

=== Quarterly Subscription Spanning Three Months

A customer is invoiced *$90.00* for a quarterly plan covering `2026-01-01` to `2026-04-01`, which is 90 days. The daily amount is $1.00:

[cols="1,1,1", options="header"]
|===
| Period | Days | Revenue

| `2026-01`
| 31
| $31.00

| `2026-02`
| 28
| $28.00

| `2026-03`
| 31
| $31.00

| *Total*
| *90*
| *$90.00*
|===

At the end of January, $31.00 is recognized and $59.00 is still deferred.

=== Rounding

A customer is invoiced *$100.00* for `2026-04-01` to `2026-05-01` (30 days). Each of the first 29 entries is $3.333333333 (100 ÷ 30, rounded to nine decimals). The last entry, on `2026-04-30`, is $3.333333343, so that the 30 entries add up to exactly $100.00.

=== One-Time Setup Fee

A customer is invoiced a fixed *$250.00* setup fee with a service start date of `2026-04-15`. Aviate creates an immediate schedule with one entry of $250.00 in period `2026-04`. The first run on or after `2026-04-15` recognizes the full amount and closes the schedule.

=== Month-End Close

On `2026-05-02`, the finance team closes April:

. The daily run on `2026-04-30` or later has recognized all April entries.
. `GET /v1/revenue/periods/2026-04/summary` shows the recognized total for April with a deferred amount of 0.
. `POST /v1/revenue/periods/2026-04/close` returns `204 No Content`.
. `GET /v1/revenue/periods?closed=true` now lists `2026-04` with the closing date and user.

If a subscription had been cancelled in the middle of April, its remaining April days would still be unrecognized. Step 3 would then be rejected with `Cannot close period 2026-04: N unrecognized entries remain`, and after review the team would close the month with `force=true`.

=== Mid-Period Cancellation

A $31.00 schedule covers `2026-01-01` to `2026-02-01`. By `2026-01-15`, $15.00 has been recognized. The subscription is then cancelled with effect from `2026-01-16`, and Kill Bill credits the unused $16.00.

* Aviate records a reversal entry of −$16.00, ends the schedule on `2026-01-16` and closes it.
* Days after `2026-01-16` are not recognized.
* The unrecognized January days stay in period `2026-01`, so closing January requires `force=true`.
* The reversal entry has no accounting period, so it does not appear in the January period summary.
