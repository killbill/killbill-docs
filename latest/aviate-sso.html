Aviate Single Sign-On

Premium This is a Aviate feature.

Overview

With single sign-on (SSO), the people of your organization sign in to Aviate with the identity provider (IdP) your company already uses, such as Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, Auth0 or Keycloak. Aviate supports any IdP that speaks OpenID Connect (OIDC) or SAML 2.0.

With SSO:

  • People sign in with their company login: there is no separate Aviate password to create or remember.

  • Your IdP stays in charge of who can sign in: someone you remove from your IdP can no longer sign in to Aviate.

  • Every email address on your company domain must sign in through your IdP. Aviate password sign-up is refused for that domain.

  • Members still get a role, which decides what they can do (see Aviate Access Control).

The Aviate support team sets up SSO on behalf of your organization, including the organization itself and its owner: nobody needs to create an Aviate login first. You provide the details, configure Aviate in your IdP, and prove that you own your email domain.

Join Modes

You choose how people from your company become members of your Aviate organization.

Mode Behavior

Auto-join (default)

Anyone your IdP authenticates with an email on your domain becomes a member on their first sign-in, with the organization’s default role. You then change each member’s role as needed.

Invite-only

Only people you invite can sign in. You invite them by email from the Members tab, with a role, as described in Aviate Members. Their first sign-in through your IdP accepts the invitation. Anyone else is refused, even if your IdP authenticates them.

The default role is aviate_read unless you ask for another one. In auto-join mode, keep it narrow: anyone in your IdP gets it. It only applies to people who join without an invitation. People you invited get the role you picked for them, in both modes.

Before You Start

  • You know who will be the owner of your organization in Aviate: the person who manages members and their roles. The Aviate support team creates the organization and makes this person its owner; they don’t need an Aviate login beforehand.

  • You can add a DNS record to your email domain, or you know who can.

  • You can create an application in your IdP, or you know who can.

  • The default role, and any role you plan to give members, is defined on each Kill Bill deployment your organization uses (see Built-In Roles).

Step 1: Contact the Aviate Support Team

Write to the Aviate support team at support@killbill.io: tell them you want to set up SSO for your organization, and which kind of IdP you use (OIDC or SAML). The team sends you what your IdP needs to know about Aviate:

  • For OIDC: the redirect (callback) URL.

  • For SAML: the Assertion Consumer Service (ACS) URL and the audience (SP entity ID).

Step 2: Configure Aviate in Your IdP

Create an application for Aviate in your IdP, using the values from step 1, and assign it to the people who should use Aviate.

Your IdP must send each user’s email address:

  • For OIDC, through the email claim, and mark it as verified (email_verified set to true). Aviate requests the openid email profile scopes.

  • For SAML, through an attribute of your choice, for example email or http://schemas.xmlsoap.org/ws/2005/05/identity/claims/emailaddress.

Step 3: Send Your Details

Send the Aviate support team:

Detail Example

The name of your organization

acme-corp

The owner’s work email

jane@acme.com

Your email domain(s)

acme.com

Your IdP settings, for OIDC

Issuer URL (https://acme.okta.com), client ID and client secret. Send the secret through a secure channel.

Your IdP settings, for SAML

Metadata URL (or metadata XML file), name of the email attribute, and whether Aviate should sign its requests, expect encrypted responses, or sign users out of your IdP when they sign out of Aviate.

The name of the sign-in button

Acme Okta

The join mode

Auto-join or invite-only

The default role

aviate_read

Step 4: Verify Your Domain

The Aviate support team sends you a DNS TXT record for each domain, for example:

Name Value

_aviate-sso.acme.com

aviate-sso-verification=<token>

Publish the record in your DNS and tell the team. Aviate checks it once, to confirm that your organization controls the domain. You can remove the record afterwards.

Step 5: Test and Go Live

Once the domain is verified and your IdP settings are in place, the Aviate support team asks you to test a sign-in through your IdP. When the test succeeds, SSO is turned on for your organization.

From then on, people sign in by entering their work email on the Aviate sign-in page. Aviate sends them to your IdP and back. The owner signs in the same way, and gets the aviate_owner role on their first sign-in.

Managing Members With SSO

Members who sign in through your IdP appear on the Members tab like any other member:

  • In auto-join mode, a person appears as Active, with the default role, after their first sign-in. Change their role from the Role column.

  • In invite-only mode, invite people from the Members tab before they sign in.

  • In both modes, revoking a member refuses their sign-ins, even if your IdP authenticates them.

  • Removing someone from your IdP, or from the Aviate application in your IdP, stops their sign-ins. A session already open stays valid until it expires.

To change the join mode, the default role or your IdP settings, contact the Aviate support team. Switching from auto-join to invite-only keeps the members who already joined; revoke the ones who should no longer have access. Changing the default role does not change the role of existing members.

Note
 A person who already has an Aviate password login with their work email cannot sign in through your IdP. Contact the Aviate support team if this happens.

Sign-In Errors

When Aviate refuses an SSO sign-in, the sign-in page shows one of the following codes:

Code Meaning

SSO_NOT_INVITED

Your organization is invite-only, and this person has not been invited. Invite them from the Members tab.

SSO_MEMBER_DEACTIVATED

This person’s access was revoked, or they declined the invitation.

SSO_DOMAIN_MISMATCH

The email address sent by your IdP is not on one of your verified domains.

SSO_EMAIL_NOT_VERIFIED

Your OIDC IdP did not mark the email address as verified (email_verified).

SSO_PASSWORD_ACCOUNT_EXISTS

This email address already has an Aviate password login. Contact the Aviate support team.

SSO_NOT_ACTIVE

SSO is not turned on for your organization yet.

SSO_REQUIRED

Someone tried to sign up to Aviate with a password using an email address on your domain. They must sign in through your IdP instead.
